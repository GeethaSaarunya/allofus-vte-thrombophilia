Figure 1. Cohort flow diagram. Adults with pre-enrollment clinical-risk ascertainment in the eligible All of Us EHR cohort were screened for the availability of qualifying short-read whole-genome sequencing (WGS) for targeted genetic analyses. The final WGS analysis cohort comprised 358,533 participants, including 7,553 participants with observed VTE during follow-up and 350,980 participants without a qualifying VTE record during follow-up.
