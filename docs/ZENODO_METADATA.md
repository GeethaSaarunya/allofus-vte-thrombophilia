**Description:**
Analysis code, aggregate-safe source tables, and publication figure code supporting an EHR-linked genomic cohort study of clinical VTE risk burden and inherited thrombophilia in the All of Us Research Program. Participant-level All of Us data are not included and remain available only to authorized researchers through the Researcher Workbench.
